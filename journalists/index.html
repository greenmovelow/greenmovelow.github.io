About

Timothy C. Tucker

Founder & Executive Editor

Bluesky: @exposed1.substack.com

Timothy C. Tucker is the Executive Editor of Restoring Democracy's Promise, an investigative newsroom based in Iowa. His reporting began at the local level, documenting extremist school board candidates, but quickly expanded as evidence led him into the national networks shaping those campaigns.

Living in one of the nation's hotbeds of Christian nationalism, he has focused on exposing how religion is weaponized as a political tool through dark money networks, model legislation, and coordinated influence operations. His work, cited by organizations such as the Interfaith Alliance of Iowa, frames local stories within a larger national struggle to preserve democratic institutions.

Tucker holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Iowa State University, bringing a systems-oriented, quantitative approach to investigative reporting. He is a member of Investigative Reporters & Editors (IRE) and applies engineering-discipline methodology to document-driven journalism — emphasizing measurable claims, transparent sourcing, and reproducible analysis.

Through RDP Analytics, the newsroom's civic systems analysis division, Tucker develops interactive data models, network maps, and technical explainers that translate procurement records, government contracts, and organizational structures into navigable systems — so the public can audit what governments build.
