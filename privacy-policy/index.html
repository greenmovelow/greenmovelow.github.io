Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective Date: February 10, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Our Commitment to Source Protection

As an investigative newsroom, source protection is foundational to everything we do. We do not voluntarily disclose the identity of a confidential source. If we receive a subpoena, warrant, or other legal demand, we will challenge it to the fullest extent permitted by law and seek to disclose only what is legally required. We use encrypted communication channels, minimize the data we collect, and design our systems to protect the people who trust us with sensitive information.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Restoring Democracy's Promise ("RDP," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our main website at restoring-democracy.org, use the investigations publication at investigations.restoring-democracy.org, subscribe to our newsletter, submit tips, or otherwise interact with our organization. For the Substack-hosted investigations publication, this policy covers the processing RDP controls as its creator; Substack separately controls some platform-level processing under its own policy.

We are committed to transparency in our journalism and in how we handle your data. This policy is written in plain language because we believe privacy policies should be understandable, not just legally defensible.

2. Who We Are

Restoring Democracy's Promise is an independent investigative newsroom project focused on accountability journalism and civic systems analysis. We investigate how power is built and exercised through systems—surveillance, identity verification, data-sharing pipelines, governance mechanisms, contracting, and enforcement workflows. Our work is funded independently and we do not accept advertising revenue.

3. Information We Collect

We collect as little information as necessary to operate our newsroom and serve our readers. Here is what we may collect and how:

3.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Newsletter subscriptions: If you subscribe to our Substack newsletter, RDP can access the email address you provide for managing the publication and sending newsletters. Substack also processes subscription information under the roles described below; RDP does not receive or control everything Substack processes.
  • Tips and correspondence: If you contact us via email, Signal, or other communication channels, we receive whatever information you choose to share. We strongly encourage the use of encrypted channels (Signal, ProtonMail) for sensitive communications.
  • Contact form submissions: If you use any contact form on our website, we collect the information you submit (typically name and email address).

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

Different services operate on the main RDP website and the Substack-hosted investigations publication. The distinctions below explain who may process information and why.

Main RDP website

  • Aggregate analytics: On ordinary public-content pages, RDP uses GoatCounter for cookieless aggregate measurement of page readership, referring websites, and broad technical usage patterns. GoatCounter does not place cookies or persistent browser identifiers, and RDP does not use it to construct individual browsing histories.
  • Site delivery and security: Netlify and Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP address, requested URL, referral information, browser or device characteristics, user agent, traffic patterns, and security-event information to deliver the site, maintain performance, prevent abuse, and respond to attacks.
  • Cloudflare Precursor: When enabled, Cloudflare may inject a client-side security script that evaluates technical and session-level behavioral signals to distinguish legitimate readers from automated activity. The results may update security state stored in Cloudflare's cf_clearance cookie. RDP uses this processing for website security, bot detection, and abuse prevention—not advertising, political targeting, or reader profiling.
  • Interactive pages: Certain interactive pages load the vis-network JavaScript library from jsDelivr. When a browser requests that library, jsDelivr may receive ordinary connection data such as the IP address, user agent, requested resource, and timestamp. jsDelivr supplies a software file; RDP does not use it as an analytics service.

Substack-hosted investigations subdomain

investigations.restoring-democracy.org is hosted and delivered through Substack. Substack may process account, subscription, engagement, device, browser, IP-address, cookie, payment-status, and interaction information. What it processes depends on how a reader uses the publication. This does not mean RDP receives or controls every item Substack processes.

Substack states that it acts as a data controller when it processes information for its own purposes. It may also act as a processor for creators, while creators remain responsible for their own processing and disclosures. Substack's platform-level practices are described in its Privacy Policy; information processed in providing publication services to RDP may also be connected with RDP's operation of the publication.

Google Analytics on the investigations subdomain

RDP has enabled Google Analytics for the Substack-hosted investigations publication, and Substack automatically loads the connected Google analytics or tagging tool on publication pages. Google Analytics may process page views, referral sources, approximate geography, browser and device information, session activity, and actions such as subscription conversions. It may use browser identifiers and first-party analytics cookies such as _ga. RDP uses this information to understand readership and improve the publication—not for advertising, retargeting, political targeting, voter profiling, or selling reader information. Google Analytics is not described here as anonymous or cookieless.

RDP does not maintain a separate internal database of individual visitor browsing histories and does not sell reader data. Infrastructure and platform providers may still process the technical information described above under their respective roles and policies.

3.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation to read our investigations.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or data brokers.
  • We do not collect financial or payment information through our website (donations, if any, are processed through third-party platforms with their own privacy policies).
  • RDP does not use browser fingerprinting or behavioral information to identify readers, build persistent reader profiles, target advertising, or track people across websites. Cloudflare may process technical and session-level signals solely to deliver and protect the site and detect bots, abuse, fraud, or security threats.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following limited purposes:

  • Delivering journalism: To publish and distribute our investigations, reports, and newsletters to subscribers.
  • Improving our website: To improve site performance, accessibility, and content organization.
  • Responding to correspondence: To reply to questions, feedback, and tips sent to us by readers and sources.
  • Protecting our operations: To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or security threats to our website infrastructure.

5. What We Will Never Do

We make the following commitments regarding your data:

  • We will never sell your personal information. Not to advertisers, data brokers, political organizations, or any other third party.
  • We will never share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We will not voluntarily disclose the identity of a confidential source to law enforcement or any other party. If we receive a subpoena, warrant, or other legal demand, we will challenge it to the fullest extent permitted by law and seek to disclose only what is legally required.
  • We will never use reader data for political targeting, voter profiling, or any form of surveillance—the very practices our journalism is designed to expose.
  • We will never display third-party advertising on our website or within our newsletters.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on a limited number of third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy governing data they process:

  • Substack (privacy policy) — Hosts and delivers the investigations publication and provides newsletter, account, subscription, payment-status, and engagement features. Substack controls some processing for its own purposes and may process other information while providing services to RDP. RDP does not receive or control all information Substack processes.
  • Google Analytics (Google Privacy Policy; how Google uses information from partner sites) — Provides readership and conversion measurement enabled by RDP on the Substack-hosted investigations subdomain. It may use analytics cookies and browser identifiers there.
  • GoatCounter (privacy information) — Provides cookieless aggregate readership measurement without advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • Netlify (privacy policy) — Provides static website hosting and deployment infrastructure.
  • GitHub (privacy statement) — Hosts RDP's public source-code repository and connects with Netlify for website deployment. Ordinary visits to the published website do not normally require a direct request to GitHub.
  • Cloudflare (privacy policy) — Provides content delivery, traffic management, DDoS mitigation, bot detection, challenges, and other security services, including Precursor.
  • jsDelivr (privacy policy) — Delivers the vis-network JavaScript library on certain interactive main-site pages and may receive ordinary connection data. RDP does not use jsDelivr as analytics.
  • Tailwind CSS and brand fonts — These are self-hosted static assets, not third-party runtime services.

We select service providers carefully and limit our use of third-party tools. We do not integrate social media tracking pixels, advertising networks, or data enrichment services.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The main website and the investigations subdomain do not use the same technologies. The main site's GoatCounter implementation is cookieless, but that does not mean the Substack-hosted investigations subdomain is entirely cookieless.

GoatCounter
Purpose: Aggregate readership measurement
How it operates on this site: Cookieless aggregate measurement
Cloudflare security cookies
Purpose: Site delivery, bot and attack prevention
How it operates on this site: Necessary for site delivery and security
Substack
Purpose: Delivering the investigations publication and its account, subscription, performance, and functionality features
How it operates: Substack uses necessary, performance, and functionality cookies on the investigations subdomain
Google Analytics
Purpose: Readership, session, and subscription-conversion measurement
How it operates: RDP enabled it on the investigations subdomain; it may use browser identifiers and first-party analytics cookies such as _ga

RDP does not use analytics cookies on the main site, and it does not use advertising cookies, advertising pixels, retargeting pixels, data broker scripts, or cross-site tracking pixels. GoatCounter provides limited aggregate readership measurement on ordinary public-content pages without setting cookies or persistent browser identifiers. Cloudflare may set cookies that are necessary for site security and delivery.

GoatCounter is limited to ordinary public-content pages and is not loaded on sensitive source-protection or other operational pages. Blocking GoatCounter must not interfere with reading the website; published content does not depend on the analytics script.

  • Aggregate analytics: GoatCounter is used for limited readership measurement and site improvement on ordinary public-content pages. It does not create persistent reader profiles.
  • Security cookies and session verification: Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies, including cf_clearance, to record security verification, prevent repeated challenges, detect automated abuse, and protect the website. When Precursor is enabled, Cloudflare may periodically reassess session-level signals and update this security state during a visit. These mechanisms are used for security and site delivery, not advertising.
  • Independent security layer: Cloudflare security processing may operate on Cloudflare-proxied pages even where GoatCounter is intentionally disabled.
  • Substack platform cookies: Substack uses its own necessary, performance, and functionality cookies on the investigations subdomain. Substack says it provides more detailed cookie controls in select jurisdictions; the controls available to a particular reader may differ by location.
  • RDP-enabled analytics: Google Analytics may use analytics cookies and identifiers on the investigations subdomain. We do not claim that Substack always blocks Google Analytics until a reader gives consent. This disclosure does not replace consent where applicable law requires it.
  • No advertising tracking: We do not use retargeting pixels, social media tracking pixels, or advertising networks.

Your choices: Available cookie controls may differ by jurisdiction. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, use privacy tools to limit analytics requests, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking GoatCounter does not prevent access to published journalism. Blocking required Cloudflare security mechanisms may cause you to receive a challenge or may prevent a suspicious or unverifiable request from reaching the site.

8. Data Retention

  • Newsletter data: We retain your email address for as long as you are subscribed. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter, and your address will be removed from our active mailing list.
  • GoatCounter statistics: GoatCounter stores aggregate readership statistics rather than persistent reader profiles.
  • Google Analytics information: Retention for Google Analytics information associated with the investigations subdomain follows RDP's configured Analytics settings and Google's applicable policies. We do not state a fixed period here because the configured period requires owner verification.
  • Substack information: Substack retains information under its own policy and according to the services it provides. RDP does not control retention for information held solely by Substack.
  • Cloudflare security information: RDP does not maintain a separate database containing individual Precursor behavioral-event streams. Cloudflare retains security logs and metadata under Cloudflare's settings, contractual terms, and retention practices.
  • Correspondence: We minimize retention of emails, tips, and messages, keeping them only for as long as they are relevant to active reporting or ongoing investigations and securely deleting them when they are no longer needed.
  • Source communications: Communications from confidential sources are handled with the highest level of care. We store such communications using encrypted methods and retain them only as long as necessary to support the relevant investigation.

9. Geographic Scope and International Processing

RDP is based in the United States, and our journalism is primarily directed to U.S. readers. Substack, Google, and other service providers may process information in the United States and other jurisdictions under their applicable policies. Privacy rights and available platform or cookie controls may vary by location.

10. Source & Whistleblower Protection

Protecting people who share information with us is a core editorial and ethical obligation. If you are a source or potential whistleblower, please note the following:

  • Use encrypted channels: For sensitive communications, use Signal (username: restoredemocracy.27) or encrypted email at restoringdemocracyspromise.scoop255@passmail.net. Avoid transmitting sensitive material over standard, unencrypted email.
  • Minimal metadata: We encourage sources to minimize digital footprints when contacting us—consider using a dedicated device or public Wi-Fi, and avoid sending documents with identifying metadata when possible.
  • Editorial firewall: Source identities are known only to the editor directly handling the investigation. We compartmentalize source information and do not maintain centralized source databases.
  • Legal protection: We will invoke available reporter's privilege protections and shield law provisions to resist compelled disclosure of source identities to the fullest extent permitted by law.

11. Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, we voluntarily honor the following requests, while also providing any rights required by applicable law:

  • Access: You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal data. We will comply unless the data is required for an ongoing investigation or legal obligation.
  • Unsubscribe: You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at compliance@restoring-democracy.org. We will respond within 45 days (or sooner where required by law).

RDP can address requests concerning information it controls. For information controlled solely by Substack or Google, please contact the appropriate provider through its privacy policy or privacy-request process. We will offer reasonable assistance in identifying the appropriate channel, but we cannot fulfill a request on a provider's behalf.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at compliance@restoring-democracy.org.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the information we collect and store. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption across our entire website.
  • Use of encrypted communication tools (Signal, ProtonMail) for sensitive editorial correspondence.
  • Limited access controls—only authorized editorial staff have access to subscriber data or source communications.
  • Regular review of third-party service providers and their security practices.

No system is perfectly secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage the use of encrypted channels for any sensitive communications.

14. State-Specific Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Where applicable, California residents have the right to: know what personal information we collect and how it is used; request deletion of personal information; opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal data); and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To submit a request, email compliance@restoring-democracy.org with the subject line "California Privacy Request."

Residents of Other States with Comprehensive Privacy Laws

Where applicable, several U.S. states provide consumer privacy rights (including rights to access, correct, delete, and opt out of certain processing such as targeted advertising). We do not engage in targeted advertising, profiling for marketing, or the sale of personal data. To exercise any applicable rights, contact us at compliance@restoring-democracy.org.

Iowa Residents

Where applicable, Iowa's Consumer Data Protection Act provides Iowa residents rights regarding personal data processing. As a newsroom with significant Iowa coverage, we are committed to honoring applicable rights and our voluntary privacy commitments. Iowa residents may contact us at compliance@restoring-democracy.org to exercise their data rights.
