# Editorial Team

An independent investigative newsroom following evidence across surveillance, money, government power, civil rights, immigration, voting rights, and education.

## Lead Profile

### Timothy C. Tucker

Founder & Executive Editor

Timothy C. Tucker is the Founder and Executive Editor of Restoring Democracy’s Promise, an independent investigative newsroom based in Iowa. His reporting began at the local level, documenting extremist school board candidates, but expanded as evidence led into the national networks shaping those campaigns.

Working from one of the country’s active testing grounds for Christian nationalism, Tucker focuses on how religion is weaponized through dark money networks, model legislation, and coordinated influence operations. His work connects local stories to the larger national struggle over democratic accountability and institutional capture.

Trained as an electrical engineer and a member of Investigative Reporters & Editors, Tucker brings a systems-oriented, evidence-driven approach to journalism—emphasizing measurable claims, transparent sourcing, and reproducible analysis.

Through [RDP Analytics](https://restoring-democracy.org/analytics/), the newsroom’s research and methods division, he develops interactive data models, network maps, and technical explainers that help the public inspect how power is organized, financed, and deployed.

## Coverage Areas

### Surveillance Systems

Identity verification, public-private data systems, and the machinery of warrantless surveillance.

### Dark Money & Influence Networks

Donor pipelines, model legislation, and coordinated influence shaping policy from behind the curtain.

### Government Accountability

Public records, procurement trails, conflicts of interest, and the architecture of state power.

### Civil Rights & Immigration

Due process, identity systems, administrative barriers, and policies that shape the rights and mobility of immigrants and marginalized communities.

### Voting Rights & Election Systems

Voter access, election administration, verification systems, and the laws and institutions governing democratic participation.

### Church-State & Education

Christian nationalism in schools, faith-based governance, and the intersection of religion and public policy.
