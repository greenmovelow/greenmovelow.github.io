RDP Analytics

Research & Methods Division

RDP Analytics is the research and methods division of Restoring Democracy’s Promise — turning public records, procurement trails, timelines, and government filings into structured findings that accountability journalism can test, publish, and defend.

Our approach: document-first modeling, auditable by design, and calibrated to the record — not narrative convenience.

Division Overview

What RDP Analytics Does

Investigative journalism produces stories. RDP Analytics builds the underlying methods that make those stories verifiable — translating document stacks into structured models, and models into exhibits the public can inspect, follow, and challenge.

Evidence-First Modeling

Analysis begins with primary records — statutes, contracts, MOUs, procurement files, court documents, and oversight materials. Models are built from the documentary record, not from narrative preference.

Auditable by Design

Major findings are traceable to source records, disclosed assumptions, and a documented analytical process built for scrutiny.

Adversarial Hardening

Before publication, core claims are tested against the strongest counterarguments available. The goal is not just to persuade, but to survive review.

Reproducible Methods

Where methods can be shared without exposing proprietary implementation, they are documented clearly enough for others to inspect, understand, and independently evaluate the logic.

Core Capabilities

RDP Analytics turns investigative leads into models. Missing reports, sudden funding shifts, procedural gaps, and timing anomalies can be tested against what should be statistically possible — not just what officials say happened.

Application 01 Governance Accountability

Shutdown Accountability Matrix

When procedural responsibility is diffused across institutions, polling alone cannot resolve the structure of the event. The Accountability Matrix models influence, sequence, and responsibility share across actors — quantifying pressure without confusing it with legal blame.

Findings are presented with disclosed assumptions and uncertainty bounds so readers can evaluate the structure, not just the headline claim.

Model Specification

USG State-Space Network Algorithm (v1.3) • Q3 2025 data window
95% CI • Monte Carlo n=10,000 • p ≤ 0.05 • Convergence: 42 epochs

Application 02 Oversight Infrastructure

Oversight Monitoring

Tracks degradations in oversight capacity across agencies and watchdog infrastructure, helping identify when accountability systems are being weakened in real time.

This monitoring layer directly informs sourcing strategy and claim confidence levels across RDP's reporting. When oversight infrastructure is degraded, accountability for the systems we cover diminishes in predictable, documentable ways.

Who It Serves

RDP Analytics is built first for the newsroom’s own reporting. Where the work has broader value, we engage selectively with aligned public-interest organizations.

Aligned Organizations & Partners

Investigative Newsrooms

Document-driven reporting that needs structured analysis strong enough to support publication, challenge, and follow-on investigation.

Civic Accountability Organizations

Groups working on democratic process, public transparency, and institutional oversight that need technically credible analysis.

Academic & Research Partners

Researchers seeking methodologically serious collaboration grounded in public records and civic systems.

Pro-Democracy Coalitions

Organizations monitoring structural threats to democratic governance and institutional accountability.

Licensing & Collaboration

RDP Analytics collaborates selectively. Any engagement is reviewed for independence risks before it begins. We do not enter arrangements that would constrain the newsroom’s freedom to investigate a client, partner, or affiliated network.

Independence is not a feature offered selectively. It is a condition of engagement.
