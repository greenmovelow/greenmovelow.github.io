Division Overview

What RDP Analytics Does

Investigative journalism produces stories. RDP Analytics produces the underlying methods that make those stories verifiable—turning document stacks into structured models, and models into interactive exhibits the public can navigate and audit.

Evidence-First

Analysis begins with primary documents: statutes, MOUs, contracts, procurement records, and court filings. Models are calibrated to the documentary record, not to narrative convenience.

Transparent Methodology

Confidence levels, model parameters, and data sources are disclosed. Findings are labeled: confirmed, high-confidence, or inferred—never conflated.

Adversarial Hardening

Every analysis is stress-tested against its strongest counterarguments before publication. We write to survive hostile scrutiny.

Reproducibility

Where methodologies can be shared without disclosing proprietary implementation, we document them so other researchers can replicate our conclusions.

Faction Responsibility Network

Application 01 Governance Accountability

Shutdown Accountability Matrix

When responsibility for a government shutdown is contested across multiple legislative factions and the executive branch, polling alone cannot resolve the structural question. The Shutdown Accountability Matrix applies our USG Baseline Model to distribute weighted responsibility scores across actors based on procedural records, voting histories, and inter-faction influence patterns.

The October 2025 model (v1.3) assigned weighted scores across six actors, with a 41.5% structural responsibility share to the executive tier and 35.0% to the House Freedom Caucus. Crucially: the model quantifies structural influence—not legal blame. That distinction is stated explicitly in every output.

Model Specification

USG State-Space Network Algorithm (v1.3) • Q3 2025 data window
95% CI • Monte Carlo n=10,000 • p ≤ 0.05 • Convergence: 42 epochs

Application 02 Oversight Infrastructure

Federal Oversight Agency Monitoring

Inspector General offices, GAO, and CIGIE constitute the federal government's primary internal oversight infrastructure. RDP Analytics maintains structured tracking of IG office capacities and inter-agency reporting gaps, cross-referenced against legislative and executive actions that modify oversight mandates.

This monitoring layer directly informs sourcing strategy and claim confidence levels across RDP's reporting. When oversight infrastructure is degraded, accountability for the systems we cover diminishes in predictable, documentable ways.

Data Sources

CIGIE public reports • GAO testimony • Agency budget filings • Congressional Record

Application 03 Financial Forensics

Shell-Entity & Dark Money Network Tracing

The Pincer Movement investigation required tracing financial flows through multi-layer structures of donor-advised funds, 501(c)(4) pass-throughs, and LLC intermediaries. RDP Analytics applied cross-source reconciliation across IRS Form 990 schedules, state corporate registration data, and USASpending.gov records to reconstruct beneficial relationships that no single source disclosed.
