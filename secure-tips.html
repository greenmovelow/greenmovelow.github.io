What’s Helpful

You don’t need a perfect package. A few specifics go a long way:

  • Primary documents (emails, memos, contracts, policies, filings, minutes).
  • Key names / agencies involved, and what role they played.
  • Timeline: when you saw it, when it started, when it changed.
  • Where to verify: public links, document locations, or records requests that exist.

Safety Notes

Avoid workplace devices

If you’re contacting us about your employer or a government entity, don’t use a work phone, work email, or work laptop.

Minimize identifying details

If you’re worried about exposure, share documents first. We can ask follow-ups after we’ve verified what’s real.

Be careful with screenshots

Screenshots can include hidden metadata and identifying UI details. If you can, export or forward the underlying files instead.

If it’s urgent

Start with Signal and write URGENT in the first line, plus a one-sentence summary.

We publish receipts and protect sources where possible. If you have constraints, tell us up front and we’ll work within them.

One-Line Tip Template

Copy/paste this into Signal to start:

I have documents related to [topic/agency]. Timeframe: [dates]. What I can share: [docs]. Risk level: [low/medium/high].
