Our Two Missions

A dual-mission organization where investigative journalism is powered by proprietary analytical infrastructure — and that infrastructure is itself a resource for the civic sector.

RDP Journalism

Investigative longform reporting rooted in public records — contracts, court filings, correspondence, and oversight documentation. Interactive explainers, infographic-driven series, and document-first accountability coverage. National in scope with an Iowa-based field desk.

Structural accountability over partisan framing

RDP Analytics

Proprietary investigative modeling — AI-driven analytical systems built to detect patterns and anomalies across government data, financial flows, and institutional networks. Glass-box architecture: every output is explainable, auditable, and reproducible.

Proprietary technology · Licensed to aligned civic organizations

RDP Investigative Analytics

Our analytical division develops proprietary investigative modeling systems — including state-space-flow hybrid models for anomaly detection across government and financial data. These glass-box systems produce explainable, auditable results that power RDP's journalism and are available for licensing to aligned organizations.

We build tools for civic organizations, human rights groups, pro-democracy coalitions, and any institution committed to using analytical power responsibly. Our systems are designed for transparency — not surveillance.

Measurable claims · Transparent sourcing · Reproducible analysis

Our Standards

Methodology

We prefer primary sources: statutes, contracts, court filings, MOUs, procurement documents, and audit reports. When secondary sources are used, we triangulate and anchor to primaries. Every claim is tagged by confidence level: confirmed, high-confidence, plausible, or unconfirmed.

Full Methodology →

Corrections Policy

Corrections: We correct factual errors promptly and transparently. Substantive updates are logged; we do not stealth-edit.

Full Corrections Policy →

Independence

RDP is independent of political parties, candidates, and government agencies. We accept no advertising and no corporate sponsorships. Credibility is earned through transparent method, rigorous sourcing, and restraint in judgment. We publish receipts because the public deserves more than narratives.

Funding Transparency

RDP is currently donor-supported and reader-funded through voluntary subscriptions. We accept no government funding, no advertising revenue, and no contributions that carry editorial conditions. All future institutional funders will be disclosed publicly.

Financial disclosures are published annually.

Support Independent Investigative Journalism

Your support funds two missions: the investigative journalism that holds power accountable, and the development of analytical tools that make civic oversight scalable. RDP accepts no advertising, no corporate sponsorships, and no government funding.
