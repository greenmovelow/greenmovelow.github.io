Shutdown Accountability Analysis

STATE-SPACE ANALYSIS // OCTOBER 2025 GOVERNMENT SHUTDOWN

RDP Analytics modeled the October 2025 shutdown at the faction and procedural levels to distinguish central drivers from peripheral actors—mapping who contributed most, through which mechanisms, and with what downstream effects.

In this model, accountability refers to structurally weighted contribution within the shutdown dynamics. It is a systems measure, not a moral or legal conclusion.

Why This Matters

Conventional shutdown coverage treats institutional breakdown as diffuse chaos. This exhibit demonstrates that shutdown dynamics have structure: central drivers can be distinguished from peripheral actors, procedural leverage can be quantified, and downstream effects can be traced to specific mechanisms. The point is not a partisan verdict—it is to show that accountability within a government shutdown can be measured, documented, and subjected to scrutiny.
