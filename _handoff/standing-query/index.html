    The template is written for the expiry.

    DIAL's staff template addresses licensees whose work authorization or admission period is scheduled to expire. It has two selectable paragraphs — one for when that date falls before the license expires, one for when it falls after — and both carry the same warning.

    What the records don't show

    No record produced so far shows any Iowa professional license denied, suspended, or revoked because of a SAVE result.

    Iowa's own sworn filing described more than 350 licenses within DIAL that could not be verified and therefore could not be issued — a stall, not a revocation. This is Batch 001 of a rolling production, and the absence of a produced record is not proof that nothing happened.
