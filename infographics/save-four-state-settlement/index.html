The Four-State DHS/SAVE Settlement That Put Iowa Inside the Federal Verification Architecture

FLORIDA · OHIO · INDIANA · IOWA // N.D. FLA. // NOV 28, 2025

Iowa joined Florida, Ohio, and Indiana in a 2025 settlement requiring DHS to provide access to SAVE, execute new information-sharing agreements, and build expanded voter-verification capabilities later incorporated into a modified SAVE system a federal court vacated as unlawful.

Why this record matters

This settlement is one of the primary records showing Iowa’s entry into the DHS/SAVE side of the federal voter-data architecture. The agreement commits the state plaintiffs and DHS to execute new information-sharing and SAVE agreements, provides for modernization of SAVE, and describes expanded capabilities including bulk upload, Social Security-number search fields, and downloadable verification output.

Iowa-specific relevance

Iowa Attorney General Brenna Bird and Iowa Secretary of State Paul Pate were named plaintiffs in the settlement. The agreement is separate from Iowa’s later voter-roll transfer to the Justice Department. The exhibit is provided so readers can inspect the source document directly.

Precision Note

The agreement says each state plaintiff “may provide” a 1,000-record driver-license sample. This exhibit does not claim Iowa sent that sample. It documents the authority and framework created by the settlement.
