Bird’s 2026 Campaign Cash and RAGA: The Filings

By Timothy C. Tucker  •  Restoring Democracy’s Promise  •  Oct. 29, 2026

Updated Oct. 29, 2026  •  Filing versions as of Oct. 27, 2026

This page sets out the filing totals, tables and charts behind RDP’s article so a reader can check each figure against the reports Bird for Iowa and Willems for Iowa filed with the Iowa Ethics and Campaign Disclosure Board (IECDB).

Article: “Four of Every Five Dollars in Bird’s 2026 AG Campaign Come From RAGA,” Oct. 29, 2026.

Definitions

Terms, window and basis used on this page

Reported cash
The Schedule A total printed on each DR-2 campaign disclosure report, including any interest, refunds and returned contributions the campaign listed there. In-kind contributions (Schedule E), loans and outside spending are excluded.
Entries
Schedule A rows as printed. Bird for Iowa’s four 2026 reports contain 2,974 entries; Willems for Iowa’s contain 10,366.
Gifts
Contribution entries, each sized by its own amount. A contributor who gave several times is counted once per gift.
Contributor records
Distinct combinations of name, street address and ZIP code as printed on the filings. They approximate people but cannot confirm them. Counts by size bracket are not additive.
National committee
The RAGA Action Fund, the political arm of the Republican Attorneys General Association (RAGA), Iowa committee ID OSF50167; and DAGA Individual PAC, which shares the Democratic Attorneys General Association’s (DAGA) Washington address, Iowa committee ID 8891. “Non-national cash” is reported cash minus the national committee’s transfers.
Window
Jan. 1–Oct. 14, 2026: four consecutive DR-2 reports for each campaign (Jan. 1–May 14; May 15–26 supplemental; May 27–July 14; July 15–Oct. 14).
Basis
Shares are computed from exact cents and are dollar-weighted. Each report’s entries were summed and tied to the Schedule A total printed on the filing.
Filing versions
The latest versions on the IECDB site as of Oct. 27, 2026, identified by file name and SHA-256 hash in section 4.

5 • Method

How RDP counted

“Reported cash” is the Schedule A total printed on each DR-2 report, including any interest, refunds, and returned contributions the campaign listed there. “Contributor records” are distinct names, street addresses, and ZIP combinations as printed on the filings. They approximate people but cannot confirm them. Gift sizes use each contribution’s own amount. In-kind contributions, loans, and outside spending are excluded throughout.

  1. Transcribe. RDP transcribed every Schedule A entry on the four 2026 DR-2 reports for each campaign, using the latest amended versions on the IECDB site as of Oct. 27, 2026. Entries that span a page break were rejoined.
  2. Tie out. Each report’s entries were summed and compared with the Schedule A grand total and the summary-page Schedule A total printed on the filing. All eight reports tie to the cent.
  3. Classify. In order of precedence: a filing note saying interest or a refund of a deposit or expenditure marks a non-contribution receipt; a recorded manual decision, where one was needed; a committee ID printed on the entry marks a PAC or political committee; a business name (“DBA”) marks an other organization; trust or trustee wording marks a trust; organization keywords or suffixes mark an other organization; remaining “Last, First” names are individuals. A run stops if any entry is left unclassified.
  4. National committee. Entries printed with committee ID OSF50167 (RAGA Action Fund) for Bird and 8891 (DAGA Individual PAC) for Willems. Way Back PAC (OSF50348) is not a national committee and stays in Willems’s non-national cash.
  5. Size. Each positive contribution entry is placed in one of eight brackets by its own amount. Refund reversals and interest or refund receipts are shown but not sized.
  6. Contributor records. A record is a distinct normalized name, normalized street and five-digit ZIP code. A looser name-and-ZIP key gives a lower bound; the two keys differ by 46 individual records for Bird (2,116 vs. 2,070) and 77 for Willems (6,153 vs. 6,076). The Iowa under-$500 counts in Table 4 count individual records whose printed state is Iowa and that have at least one positive gift under $500.
  7. In-state. An entry is in-state when its printed state is IA.
  8. Verify. A separate script recomputes brackets, totals, class totals and per-report subtotals from the saved ledgers with different code; two full runs produced identical ledgers. Period shares are dollar-weighted from exact amounts, not averages of period percentages.

The three-record table compares the amount, payer and recipient, period, purpose and date in each system. No source exposes a shared bank reference, so a match is a reported counterpart, not a traced payment.

6 • Bounds

What this page does not show

  • These filings show how each campaign’s cash was made up. They do not show how RAGA or DAGA are themselves funded.
  • Different contribution patterns are not evidence of illegality, coordination, or improper influence by any donor or committee.
  • The three-record table shows what each filer reported. It does not determine which reporting rules applied to any payment.
  • The RAGA shares by period are not evidence of a fixed or agreed funding arrangement; the money arrived in transfers of different sizes, and its share varied by period.

Limits

Limits of this collection

  • The window ends Oct. 14, 2026. The RAGA Action Fund’s Iowa statement, updated Oct. 27, lists $100,000 on Oct. 19, $175,000 on Oct. 23 and $100,000 on Oct. 27. Those payments, Bird for Iowa’s supplementary report due Oct. 30 and any amendment filed after Oct. 27 are not counted here.
  • The ledgers are withheld. RDP’s transaction ledgers contain contributors’ names and addresses and are not published. This page and its CSV carry aggregates only. RDP will share the classification rules and the per-report tie-out on request.
  • Contributor records are not people. A person who moved or whose name was printed two ways can appear as two records; two people with one name and address appear as one.
  • Classification is by name pattern, printed committee ID and filing note. Entries are retained as printed, including three near-duplicate Bird entries and six Willems refund reversals; RDP’s sensitivity checks found they do not change how the two campaigns compare.
  • Dates in Tables 6 and 7 are reported dates. RDP obtained no bank statements, wire confirmations or invoices. The $732,000 payment falls in a quarter for which RAGA’s FEC and IRS reports were not available.
  • Cash only. In-kind contributions, loans and independent expenditures, including RAGA’s own advertising in Iowa, are not counted in any share.
  • The charts are scaled images. On a phone the smallest chart labels are hard to read; Tables 1 through 5 carry every charted value.
