Discontinuity

The export records the configuration on August 17, 2026; 151 of 155 sharing rows contain no usable date. Nothing in this section can be connected in time to anything in the chronology above.

Sharing

at least 155 configured outbound sharing relationships, across 36 states and the District of Columbia. 120 also show a receiving relationship; 15 none; 12 approval required; 8 declined. 151 of 155 rows carry no usable date. No active hot-list sharing appears in either direction.

Systems and cameras

Counting convention: Top-level system rows are counted as systems; rows nested beneath them are counted as cameras. On that convention: 149 system rows (140 Mobile, 7 IP, 2 Fixed) with 560 nested cameras. Counting the 7 IP lane units as cameras rather than systems yields 142 systems and 567 cameras. Earlier RDP working notes used that shorthand.

One Mobile row is literally named “Unit number,” so any vehicle count read off that row total is off by at least one on the face of the file.
