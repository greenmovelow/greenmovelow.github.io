Evidence key

  • Open ring — configured to share (outbound)
  • Ring with inner ring — also configured to receive
  • Dashed ring — receiving shown as approval required
  • Dotted ring — receiving shown as declined
  • Struck ring — name carries an “Inactive” prefix; meaning not defined
  • Filled node — Des Moines, the reference point
  • Dashed connector — configured to share. Not data sent.
  • Open rings and dashed connectors show settings in the City's own platform export. They do not show that any plate record was sent or viewed. No connector is drawn until you select an agency, and no connector has a direction, an arrowhead or any motion.

What this table is, and is not

Every row below is a setting in the City's own platform export. Configuration is not evidence that any plate record was transferred, queried, viewed or acted on. Outbound / inbound labels are inferred from the vendor's own user-guide terminology (Share With / Accept Shares From). The export itself does not label direction. Reasonable inference, not a stated fact. The export is filtered, so 155 is a floor on configured relationships, not the total the platform holds. “Has system,” “Retention,” the “(F)” name prefix on 51 rows and the “Inactive” prefix on 5 rows are not defined in any located documentation, and this exhibit does not guess at them.

Source: Mobile Video Addendum, §4.5 License Plate Recognition Data · 20 Nov 2023 · page 9 of the 25-page clerk's file

License plate recognition (“LPR”) data collected by Customer is considered Customer Data (as defined in the Master Agreement) and is therefore subject to the Customer's own retention policy. Other similarly situated LEAs may similarly opt to share their LPR data with Customer using Vigilant VehicleManager.

Caveat: The 25-page file is the City Clerk's roll-call file as produced in August 2026. Whether these pages were before council members on November 20, 2023 is not established.
