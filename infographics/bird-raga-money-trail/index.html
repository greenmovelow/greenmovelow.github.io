The Structure Behind the Money

The structure behind the money.

Five distinct entities. Arrows show disclosed, entity-to-entity transfers and the periods they cover.

Flow diagram. Concord Fund gave $7 million to the RAGA 527 across tax years 2021–2023. The RAGA 527 transferred more than $12.6 million to the RAGA Action Fund in 2025. The RAGA Action Fund gave $3,525,000 to Bird for Iowa between December 2025 and May 2026. Separately, the Rule of Law Defense Fund recorded a $1.2 million political expenditure and about $697,000 in shared expenses with RAGA in tax year 2024. Brenna Bird is listed as a RAGA Executive Committee member and an RLDF director.
