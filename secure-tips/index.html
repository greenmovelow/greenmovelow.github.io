Secure Tips

If you have documents, first-hand information, or context relevant to our reporting, you can reach RDP here. You do not need the whole story. We verify information independently.

Last updated October 6, 2026

Before you contact us: If this concerns your employer or agency, avoid work phones, computers, accounts and networks, as well as ordinary email, calls or text messages. Use Signal for anything sensitive. No contact method is risk-free.

How to reach us

Preferred for sensitive tips

Signal

Messages and files sent through Signal are end-to-end encrypted. If being identified could put you at risk, say so before sending files so we can discuss the safest practical way to proceed.

For low-risk tips

Email

Use email for tips that would not put you at risk if exposed, or to arrange another way to reach us. Standard email is not end-to-end encrypted, so do not use it, or a work account, for sensitive material.

Confidentiality and anonymity

If you want confidentiality or anonymity, or want to speak on background or off the record, tell us before you share sensitive details. Those terms can mean different things, so we agree on them explicitly rather than assume them.

What helps us verify a tip

  • Documents or records: original files, emails, memos, contracts, filings, meeting minutes or links to public records.
  • What happened: a plain description of the event, decision, practice or discrepancy you think matters.
  • Who can confirm it: names, agencies, offices, vendors or records that can independently confirm the information.
  • Timeline: key dates and what changed, especially if records were revised or an action is ongoing.

Reducing your risk

  • Avoid workplace systems. Do not store or send the material through work devices, accounts, cloud storage or networks when practical.
  • Share only what is needed at first. You can begin with a short description. If exposure is a concern, do not send identifying details until we have discussed how to proceed.
  • Preserve originals. Original files and metadata can matter as evidence. Do not alter them just to contact us. If a file itself could identify you, contact us first and we can discuss transfer.
  • If timing matters. Use Signal and explain the deadline or timing issue in your first message.

A first message

Copy this into Signal or email and fill in what you can.

I have information about [topic/agency].
What happened: [one or two sentences].
What I can provide: [documents / records / first-hand account].
Timeframe: [dates].
Risk if identified: [low / medium / high / unsure].
Can RDP identify me as a source? [No / Only after we discuss it / Yes]

We can ask follow-up questions after we establish a safe way to communicate.
