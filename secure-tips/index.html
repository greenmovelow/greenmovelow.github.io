Secure Tips

Sources are protected. Always.

If you have documents, context, or first-hand information relevant to an investigation, we welcome tips. Use the secure methods below.

Important: Avoid sensitive material over standard email, SMS, or workplace devices. When in doubt, start with Signal.

Recommended Contact Methods
Best End-to-end encrypted

Signal

Strong end-to-end encryption. Preferred first contact. Enable disappearing messages for added protection.

What's Helpful

You don't need a perfect package.

Primary Documents

Emails, memos, contracts, policies, filings, meeting minutes.

Key Names & Agencies

Who was involved and what role they played.

Timeline

When you saw it, when it started, when it changed.

Where to Verify

Public links, document locations, or records requests that exist.

Safety Notes

Tell us your constraints up front.

Avoid workplace devices

Don't use a work phone, email, or laptop when contacting us about your employer or a government entity.

Minimize identifying details

If you're worried about exposure, share documents first. We'll ask follow-ups after verifying.

Be careful with screenshots

Screenshots can contain hidden metadata. Export or forward the underlying files when possible.

If it's urgent

Signal first. Write URGENT in the first line with a one-sentence summary.

One-Line Tip Template

Copy into Signal to start:

I have documents related to [topic/agency].
Timeframe: [dates].
What I can share: [description].
Risk level: [low / medium / high].

No tip is too small. No constraint is too complicated.
