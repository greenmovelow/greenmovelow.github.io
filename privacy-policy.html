Our Commitment to Source Protection

As an investigative newsroom, source protection is foundational to everything we do. We do not voluntarily disclose the identity of a confidential source. If we receive a subpoena, warrant, or other legal demand, we will challenge it to the fullest extent permitted by law and seek to disclose only what is legally required. We use encrypted communication channels, minimize the data we collect, and design our systems to protect the people who trust us with sensitive information.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Restoring Democracy's Promise ("RDP," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our website at restoring-democracy.org, subscribe to our newsletter, submit tips, or otherwise interact with our organization.

We are committed to transparency in our journalism and in how we handle your data. This policy is written in plain language because we believe privacy policies should be understandable, not just legally defensible.

2. Who We Are

Restoring Democracy's Promise is an independent investigative newsroom project focused on accountability journalism and civic systems analysis. We investigate how power is built and exercised through systems—surveillance, identity verification, data-sharing pipelines, governance mechanisms, contracting, and enforcement workflows. Our work is funded independently and we do not accept advertising revenue.

3. Information We Collect

We collect as little information as necessary to operate our newsroom and serve our readers. Here is what we may collect and how:

3.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Newsletter subscriptions: If you subscribe to our Substack newsletter, we receive the email address you provide. This data is held by Substack, Inc. on our behalf.
  • Tips and correspondence: If you contact us via email, Signal, or other communication channels, we receive whatever information you choose to share. We strongly encourage the use of encrypted channels (Signal, ProtonMail) for sensitive communications.
  • Contact form submissions: If you use any contact form on our website, we collect the information you submit (typically name and email address).

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

We do not use analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics), advertising pixels, or retargeting technology on this website.

However, like nearly all websites, some information may be processed automatically by infrastructure providers and third-party asset hosts:

  • Server logs (hosting/security): Our web hosting and security providers (such as GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may automatically process standard server log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This information is typically used for site delivery, performance, and security (for example, abuse prevention and DDoS mitigation).
  • Third-party asset requests: Our site may load certain assets (such as CSS/JS libraries and fonts) from third parties (for example, Tailwind CDN and Google Fonts). Those providers may receive standard technical request data (such as IP address and user agent) when your browser loads those resources.

3.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation to read our investigations.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or data brokers.
  • We do not collect financial or payment information through our website (donations, if any, are processed through third-party platforms with their own privacy policies).
  • We do not use fingerprinting technologies to identify individual visitors.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following limited purposes:

  • Delivering journalism: To publish and distribute our investigations, reports, and newsletters to subscribers.
  • Improving our website: To improve site performance, accessibility, and content organization.
  • Responding to correspondence: To reply to questions, feedback, and tips sent to us by readers and sources.
  • Protecting our operations: To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or security threats to our website infrastructure.

5. What We Will Never Do

We make the following commitments regarding your data:

  • We will never sell your personal information. Not to advertisers, data brokers, political organizations, or any other third party.
  • We will never share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We will not voluntarily disclose the identity of a confidential source to law enforcement or any other party. If we receive a subpoena, warrant, or other legal demand, we will challenge it to the fullest extent permitted by law and seek to disclose only what is legally required.
  • We will never use reader data for political targeting, voter profiling, or any form of surveillance—the very practices our journalism is designed to expose.
  • We will never display third-party advertising on our website or within our newsletters.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on a limited number of third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy governing data they process:

  • Substack (privacy policy) — Hosts our newsletter and manages subscriber email addresses.
  • GitHub Pages / Cloudflare — Hosts our website and provides content delivery and security services.
  • Tailwind CDN — Serves site styling assets via cdn.tailwindcss.com.
  • Google Fonts — Serves typefaces used on our website (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com).

We select service providers carefully and limit our use of third-party tools. We do not integrate social media tracking pixels, advertising networks, or data enrichment services.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking on this website.

That said, depending on configuration, certain cookies or similar technologies may be used for site security and stability, and some third-party asset hosts may receive technical request data:

  • Cookies we intentionally use: We do not intentionally set cookies for marketing, advertising, or analytics.
  • Strictly necessary security cookies: Our infrastructure providers (for example, Cloudflare) may set cookies or use similar mechanisms to protect the site from abuse (for example, bot detection and DDoS mitigation). These are used for security, not advertising.
  • Third-party asset requests: If your browser loads assets from providers like Google Fonts or Tailwind CDN, those providers may receive standard technical request information (such as IP address and user agent).

Your choices: You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some security protections, but it should not prevent you from reading our investigations.

8. Data Retention

  • Newsletter data: We retain your email address for as long as you are subscribed. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter, and your address will be removed from our active mailing list.
  • Server/security logs: Hosting and security providers may retain logs under their own retention policies and settings. We do not maintain a separate, centralized database of visitor browsing history.
  • Correspondence: We retain emails, tips, and messages for as long as they are relevant to active reporting or ongoing investigations, and securely delete them when they are no longer needed.
  • Source communications: Communications from confidential sources are handled with the highest level of care. We store such communications using encrypted methods and retain them only as long as necessary to support the relevant investigation.

9. Source & Whistleblower Protection

Protecting people who share information with us is a core editorial and ethical obligation. If you are a source or potential whistleblower, please note the following:

  • Use encrypted channels: For sensitive communications, use Signal (username: restoredemocracy.27) or encrypted email at restoringdemocracyspromise.scoop255@passmail.net. Avoid transmitting sensitive material over standard, unencrypted email.
  • Minimal metadata: We encourage sources to minimize digital footprints when contacting us—consider using a dedicated device or public Wi-Fi, and avoid sending documents with identifying metadata when possible.
  • Editorial firewall: Source identities are known only to the editor directly handling the investigation. We compartmentalize source information and do not maintain centralized source databases.
  • Legal protection: We will invoke available reporter's privilege protections and shield law provisions to resist compelled disclosure of source identities to the fullest extent permitted by law.

10. Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, we respect the following rights:

  • Access: You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal data. We will comply unless the data is required for an ongoing investigation or legal obligation.
  • Unsubscribe: You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at editor@restoring-democracy.org. We will respond within 45 days (or sooner where required by law).

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at editor@restoring-democracy.org.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the information we collect and store. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption across our entire website.
  • Use of encrypted communication tools (Signal, ProtonMail) for sensitive editorial correspondence.
  • Limited access controls—only authorized editorial staff have access to subscriber data or source communications.
  • Regular review of third-party service providers and their security practices.

No system is perfectly secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage the use of encrypted channels for any sensitive communications.

13. State-Specific Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect and how it is used; request deletion of your personal information; opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal data); and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To submit a request, email editor@restoring-democracy.org with the subject line "California Privacy Request."

Residents of Other States with Comprehensive Privacy Laws

Several U.S. states provide consumer privacy rights (including rights to access, correct, delete, and opt out of certain processing such as targeted advertising). We do not engage in targeted advertising, profiling for marketing, or the sale of personal data. To exercise any applicable rights, contact us at editor@restoring-democracy.org.

Iowa Residents

Iowa's Consumer Data Protection Act (effective January 1, 2025) grants Iowa residents rights regarding personal data processing. As a newsroom with significant Iowa coverage, we are committed to honoring these rights. Iowa residents may contact us at editor@restoring-democracy.org to exercise their data rights.

14. Changes to This Policy

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes that affect how we handle personal data, we will provide notice through our website or newsletter.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
